Jianxiong Li.

Hi! I study embodied AI and reinforcement learning. My dream is to develop robots that are universally deployable across diverse real-world environments.

I completed my PhD at AIR, Tsinghua University in July 2026, advised by Prof. Xianyuan Zhan and Prof. Ya-Qin Zhang.

Previously, I was a Top Seed at ByteDance Seed Robotics (December 2025–July 2026), working closely with Xiao Ma. I received my bachelor's degree in 2021 from the School of Mechanical Engineering at Xi'an Jiaotong University, where I worked on mechanical design and robotics projects.
